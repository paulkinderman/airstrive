Privacy

Last updated October 30, 2026

In short: your moments are private unless you choose who can see them. AirStrive has no ads and no trackers, and never sells or shares your data. You can export everything or delete your account at any time from Settings.

What we keep

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your email address and a sign-in identifier. If Apple or Google shares your first name, we only use it to suggest a username.
  • Your username, which other people can find by searching and see on your profile, and on any grid you share to the Atlas.
  • Your profile line, if you write one. Anyone who opens your profile can read it.
  • Your profile picture, if you add one, cropped to a 512-pixel square before it leaves your device.
  • Your friends: who you are friends with, friend requests, the friend codes behind your QR code and links, and anyone you block.
  • Your grids and moments: which squares you have revealed and finished, and the journal text, date, place, weather and people you add to a moment.
  • The interests you pick when you start, used only to suggest grids in the Atlas.
  • Grids you make, and any report you send about a shared grid, so we can review it.
  • Your photos. Photos are resized to 1600 pixels on the long edge before they leave your device. The originals stay in your photo library and are never uploaded.

Where it lives

AirStrive saves everything on your device first, so it works offline. When you are signed in, it backs up your grids, moments and photos to our database and file storage, run by Supabase, so they sync between your devices. The website and the web version of the app are hosted by Vercel.

Access rules in the database itself make sure only you can read your moments and photos. A bug in the app can't show them to someone else.

Who can see what

  • Every grid and moment is Private (just you), Friends (only friends you have accepted) or Public (anyone signed in to AirStrive who opens your profile). Everything starts Private.
  • A moment can be shared as widely as its grid, or kept narrower. Place, weather and who you were with are never shown to anyone else.
  • Friendship goes both ways: nobody becomes your friend until you have said yes, by accepting a request or by showing them your QR code or link.
  • Your profile picture is seen only by you, your friends and the people in a grid you do together. Everyone else, including people who find you in search, sees your first letter. Someone you block never sees it.
  • Your friends list isn't shown to anyone. There is no feed, and nothing you share is ranked or suggested to others.
  • If you block someone, they can't find you, see your profile or ask to be friends. They aren't told.
  • If you share a grid you made to the Atlas, the grid itself (its name and squares) becomes public, along with your username.
  • A grid you do together with friends is seen only by the people in it, never on profiles or by other friends. Everyone in it sees the board and each square's title, date, place, weather and who you were with. Your own words and photos on a square are Group (everyone in it) or Private (just you). Only friends can be invited, and only you can accept an invite.

Alexa

If you enable the AirStrive skill on an Amazon Echo and link your account, the Echo can show and read your grids and moments, and the moments your friends share with you. It sees exactly what you see in the app, can't change anything, and keeps nothing: each request is answered from your AirStrive account and photos are sent as links that expire within an hour. Amazon handles what you say to Alexa under its own privacy notice. To stop it, disable the skill in the Alexa app.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad trackers.
  • No third-party tracking scripts. This website and the web version of the app count page views with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, doesn't identify you and never sees your moments. The phone app has no analytics at all.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we don't use your moments or photos to train AI models.

Export and deletion

Settings → Export my data gives you a zip with every moment as JSON, all your photos, and your Memory Lane scrapbook.

Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, every grid, moment and photo, your profile picture, and your friends list, on every device. Database backups that contain your data expire within 14 days.

In a grid you do together, what you shared with the group stays for the others when you leave it or delete your account, so their grid isn't left with holes. After you delete your account it shows as from "a former member", without your username. Your Private words and photos there are deleted with your account. When the last person leaves a grid done together, everything on it is deleted.

Signing out removes your grids and photos from that device. They stay in your account until you sign in again.

Children

AirStrive is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
