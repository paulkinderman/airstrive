Help

Need a hand? Email airstriveadmin@gmail.com and say which phone or browser you use.

Signing in

On iPhone, sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent by email. On the web, use Google or email. Use the same way of signing in on every device so your grids follow you.

Backup and sync

Everything saves on your device first, so AirStrive works offline. When you're signed in and online it backs up and syncs on its own. Settings shows when it last backed up, and Sync now or Try again starts it by hand.

Export your moments

Settings → Export my data gives you a zip with every moment as JSON, all your photos, and your Memory Lane scrapbook.

Delete your account

Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, every grid, moment and photo, and your friends list, on every device. The privacy page says what stays in grids you did together.

Report something

Each shared grid, moment and profile has a Report option in its menu. You can also email us.
