How it works

A grid of things to try, revealed one square at a time.

A grid can be about anything: running, chess, family time, a season, a city. You start with the middle squares showing. The rest stay hidden until you get there.

Pick a grid

Start with one of the six starter grids, find one in the Atlas, or make your own. Each square is a small, specific thing to do.

Finish a square

Do the thing, add a photo and a few words. Finishing a square reveals the four squares around it.

Watch it fill in

Done squares show your photos, so a filled grid becomes a mosaic of moments you actually lived.

Memory Lane

Every moment becomes a page in your scrapbook.

Memory Lane keeps every moment from every grid, one page each, with polaroids and your words in handwriting. Finish a grid and it gets a chapter cover. Export it all as a zip whenever you like.

What it leaves out

Nothing to count.

Steps

No step counts or distances. A square is done when you did the thing.

Pace

No timers, splits or personal bests. Fast or slow, it counts.

Streaks

Nothing breaks if you take a month off. The grid waits.

Leaderboards

Share moments with friends, or keep them to yourself. Nobody is ranked.
